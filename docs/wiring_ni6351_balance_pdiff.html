1. All channels DIFFERENTIAL (ni_usb_6351 defaults): AI<n> is +, AI<n+8> is −. Never land a bridge wire on AI SENSE (terminal 29).
2. Single-point ground: tie the excitation supply − to AI GND at terminal 3 once (★) — references the bridge outputs inside the ±11 V common-mode window. A second ground point makes a loop.
3. Individually shielded twisted pairs; shields land on the listed AI GND terminal at the DAQ end ONLY; open at the balance end.
4. Ranges are the driver defaults (±0.2 V pitch/yaw bridges, ±0.5 V Axial/Roll, ±10 V Excitation and Pdiff); adjust in the NI panel's Channels tab — the driver snaps to the nearest native range.
5. Pdiff: own supply per datasheet. Single-ended output: output − and supply − together to terminal 27, jumper 27→28 (AI GND)*; differential: as drawn. Enter the psi/V scale in the Channels tab (unit psid).
6. Excitation readback (AI6) is REQUIRED — freestream and balcal normalize bridge volts by live excitation. Wire it even if the supply has a panel meter.
7. Trigger (optional): digital PFI 0 = terminal 73, D GND = terminal 82; analog edge APFI 0 = terminal 13. Configure in the NI panel's Output & Trigger tab.
8. Software layout Moment ↔ Force is a pure rename (AftPitch/AftYaw/FwdPitch/FwdYaw ↔ N1/N2/Y1/Y2) — physical wiring on AI0–AI3 does not change. Select the layout in the Forces tab.

Axial and Roll each have TWO outputs on the plate: individual elements (A₁ 25 lb · Green/Violet, ℓ₁ 60 in·lb · Green/Orange) and TOTAL composites (A₂ · Green/Violet&White, ℓ₂ · Green/Orange&White). Land whichever output the balance's .vol calibration was performed with — mixing them invalidates the force reduction.
2. S₁/S₂ on the plate are the side-force elements → software channels Y1/Y2 (Force layout). N₁/N₂ load rating 100 lb; S₁/S₂ 50 lb — the Forces tab utilization bars use the .vol maxima.
3. Every element's S+ is the GREEN conductor — label both ends of each pair before pulling, or continuity-buzz at the terminal strip; six identical greens are unforgiving.
4. Excitation 10 V for this balance (5 V for the NK moment balance on page 1) — set the supply BEFORE connecting, and verify on the AI6 readback tile in the NI panel.
5. Grounding, shields, Pdiff, triggering and range notes: identical to page 1 (notes 2–7).
6. White / Gray / White-striped conductors: confirm against the nameplate under good light — Gray (S₁−) and White (N₂−) are easy to confuse in tunnel lighting.
